Buah Rasa Publisher

A personal publishing tool for the owner of the TikTok account @buah_rasa.

Free · Personal use only

What it does

Buah Rasa Publisher lets the account owner publish their own short videos — drama clips and tutorials, one per day — to their own TikTok account, @buah_rasa. It uses the official TikTok Login Kit and TikTok Content Posting API (Direct Post). No video is uploaded anywhere else, and no content is published on behalf of anyone else.

How it works

  1. The owner signs in with their own TikTok account through TikTok Login Kit.
  2. The owner picks one of their own finished videos and a caption.
  3. The app sends the video to TikTok through the Content Posting API (Direct Post), publishing it to @buah_rasa.

Privacy, in short

The app collects no data about anyone else. The only data involved is the owner's own TikTok authorization token, which is stored locally by the owner. Nothing is sold or shared with third parties, and there is no tracking. Full details: Privacy Policy.
