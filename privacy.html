Privacy Policy

Last updated: 21 November 2026

1. Who this is for

Buah Rasa Publisher is a personal tool for one person only: the owner of the TikTok account @buah_rasa (Bayu Luqman Fadilah). It does not collect, store, or process data about anyone else.

2. What data is involved

To publish a video, the tool needs only what TikTok's official Login Kit and Content Posting API require:

  • TikTok authorization (OAuth): when the owner signs in, TikTok issues an access token (and a refresh token) that lets the tool publish to @buah_rasa. These tokens are stored locally by the owner on their own machine. They are never sent to any other server.
  • Basic account info: the TikTok username (@buah_rasa) and account identifier returned by TikTok during sign-in, used only to confirm the video goes to the right account.
  • The video and caption: the owner's own video file and caption, sent directly to TikTok through the Content Posting API when the owner chooses to publish.

3. What we do NOT do

  • No data is sold, rented, or shared with any third party.
  • No tracking, analytics, or advertising is used.
  • No data about other people is collected — the tool has no access to anyone else's account, videos, or personal information.
  • No cookies are set by this website.

4. Data retention and deletion

Tokens are kept only on the owner's own machine for as long as the owner keeps using the tool. To delete all access, the owner can revoke the application's access from their TikTok settings (TikTok app → Settings → Security → Manage app permissions) or simply delete the locally stored tokens. Revoking access immediately stops the tool from publishing. This website itself stores nothing.
